How the calculation works

Delivered heat = annual units × fuel energy × space-heating share × efficiency × weather adjustment. Heat-pump electricity = its share of delivered heat ÷ seasonal COP. Backup and maintenance are then added.

Fuel energy factors: oil 10.61 kWh/L; natural gas 10.36 kWh/m³; propane 7.03 kWh/L. These are NRCan reference factors; actual fuel properties vary.
